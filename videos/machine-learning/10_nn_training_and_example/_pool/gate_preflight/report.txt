CLOSE-OUT FORM — gate:
  python3 merge_final_deck.py gate <out.pptx> <per_slide_dir> <N>
  (dispatcher line 245: argv[2]=deck, argv[3]=per-slide dir, argv[4]=N). NO lock, read-only.
  Steps (mode_gate lines 177-232): wipes+recreates <per_slide_dir>/.merge_gate with
  pages/ src/ src_png/ subdirs and its OWN LO profile at <per_slide_dir>/.merge_gate/lo;
  renders the deck to PDF in one LO session; renders every slide_{i:03d}_recreated.pptx
  found in per_slide_dir (missing => appended as None, no error); pdftoppm -png -r 150 of
  both trees; then pixel-diffs page N vs source N with PIL ImageChops.difference().getbbox().
  Prints "Mismatched pages: [list] " or "NONE — 0/N, merge is lossless" (line 229),
  then sys.exit(1 if bad else 0) (line 232).
  "0/N mismatched pages" (line 229) = for every N where BOTH V/pages/pg-{N:02d}.png and
  V/src_png/s{N:03d}-1.png exist, the two renders are the same size and have ZERO differing
  pixels => the merge is byte-lossless at the render level.
  PRE-CONDITION / DEFECT: pages whose source pptx is ABSENT are silently skipped — they are
  never compared and never added to `bad` (lines 201-209, 224-228). A gate report of 0/N can
  therefore be produced while placeholder pages that have no cert pptx on disk remain
  unverified. On this deck today 6 placeholder pages (2,3,15,18,33,34) have no
  slide_NNN_recreated.pptx anywhere: close-out must assert "34/34 sources present"
  itself; the gate cannot.
  NOTE: gate does no md5 check and no lock — it never touches the canonical deck bytes.
