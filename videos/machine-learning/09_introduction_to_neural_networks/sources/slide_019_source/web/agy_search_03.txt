## tanh is a rescaled logistic sigmoid
tanh(x) = 2*sigma(2x) - 1, where sigma(x) = 1/(1+e^{-x}).

Derivation given by the source:
  2*sigma(2x) - 1 = 2/(1+e^{-2x}) - 1 = (2 - 1 - e^{-2x})/(1+e^{-2x})
                  = (1 - e^{-2x})/(1+e^{-2x}) = (e^{2x}-1)/(e^{2x}+1)
                  = (e^x - e^{-x})/(e^x + e^{-x}) = tanh(x)

## Ranges compared
- Sigmoid range: (0,1) — strictly positive outputs (probability/confidence scale).
- Tanh range: (-1,1) — symmetric, zero-centered; passes (0,0) rather than (0,0.5),
  which keeps backpropagation gradients more balanced and generally aids convergence
  of deep networks.
