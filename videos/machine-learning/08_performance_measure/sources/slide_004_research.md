- Confusion-matrix orientation: rows = Actual, columns = Predicted as: CONFIRMED — Wikipedia, Confusion matrix, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confusion_matrix — the standard layout puts actual classes on the vertical axis and predicted classes on the horizontal axis, matching both matrices' "Predicted as" column headers and "Actual" row header.
- TP sits at Actual-Positive / Predicted-Positive, TN at Actual-Negative / Predicted-Negative, with TP and TN shaded as "Correctly Classified": CONFIRMED — Han & Kamber ch. 6 (classifier evaluation), https://www3.cs.stonybrook.edu/~cse521/ch6book.pdf — correct predictions lie on the matrix diagonal (TP, TN) and misclassifications off-diagonal (FP, FN), so the green "Correctly Classified" markers on TP/TN are standard.
- Radar mapping of the four outcomes (TP=18, FN=12, TN=60, FP=10): CONFIRMED — Han & Kamber ch. 6, https://www3.cs.stonybrook.edu/~cse521/ch6book.pdf — TP = actual positive classified positive; FN = actual positive classified negative; TN = actual negative classified negative; FP = actual negative classified positive. The slide's leaf circles (30→18/12 and 70→60/10) follow this mapping with the True/False classification row beneath.
- Counts 18 + 12 = 30 and 60 + 10 = 70: CONFIRMED — Wikipedia, Confusion matrix, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confusion_matrix — each actual-class row must sum to that class's total, so Total Positive = TP+FN = 18+12 = 30 and Total Negative = FP+TN = 10+60 = 70 are internally consistent.
- "And classification is: True / False" row labeling: CONFIRMED — Han & Kamber ch. 6, https://www3.cs.stonybrook.edu/~cse521/ch6book.pdf — a prediction is "correct"/"True" when its class matches the actual class; the slide's True/False row is a plain-English rendering of match vs. mismatch.
